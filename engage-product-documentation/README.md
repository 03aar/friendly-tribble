## 🌟 Product Summary

Engage is a unified platform that combines **performance management**, **emotional wellbeing**, and **career growth** into one seamless experience.

### What It Does
1. **Clarifies work** — Defines what matters and why
2. **Measures progress** — Tracks actions, results, outcomes
3. **Understands emotion** — Captures mood, stress, motivation
4. **Recognizes people** — Builds appreciation and visibility
5. **Drives growth** — Guides skill development

### Who It's For
- **Employees** at companies (10 to 10,000+ people)
- **Managers** who lead teams
- **Leaders** who guide organizations

### The Promise
When people feel fulfilled, they perform at their best.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

**Built on three unbreakable principles:**

1. **Simplicity** — If it's not easy to use, it won't be used
2. **Humanity** — If it doesn't make people feel good, it doesn't belong
3. **Intelligence** — If it can't guide improvement, it's incomplete

Every feature exists only if it fulfills all three.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

Engage measures success through:
- **Engagement Score** — Daily active usage
- **Wellbeing Index** — Mood trends over time
- **Recognition Rate** — Frequency of appreciation
- **Goal Completion** — Achievement velocity
- **Growth Velocity** — Skill development pace
