# Product Overview

## What is Engage?

Engage is a single, intelligent platform that combines **performance**, **emotion**, and **growth** into one unified experience.

It helps people work better, feel happier, and stay aligned whether they're part of a 10-person startup or a 10,000-person enterprise.

---

## The Core Idea

**When people feel fulfilled, they perform at their best.**

Engage is not built as a corporate HR tool. It's built as a **human system** where goals, recognition, reflection, and learning are woven together seamlessly into daily work life.

---

## What Engage Does

Engage manages and improves how people work and how they feel while doing it.

It measures performance, tracks emotional wellbeing, and connects both through AI.

### The Five Core Functions

1. **Clarifies work** — Defines what each person is doing and why it matters
2. **Measures progress** — Tracks actions, results, and outcomes
3. **Understands emotion** — Captures mood, stress, and motivation levels
4. **Recognizes people** — Builds a constant flow of appreciation and visibility
5. **Drives growth** — Learns what each person needs to improve and guides them

Engage becomes the **daily companion for productivity and happiness** — the invisible system that keeps teams balanced and moving forward.

---

## The Product Flow

When a user signs in, Engage creates a clear and personalized environment:

1. They see their **focus goals** for the week
2. They check their **mood status** and can reflect if needed
3. They **receive or give recognition** to others
4. They review **AI insights** that guide them on what's working and what's not
5. They **close the day** with a sense of clarity — they know what they achieved and how they felt doing it

Each day, Engage becomes more intelligent, more adaptive, and more personalized.

---

## The Product Journey

Engage follows a clear, emotional journey for every user:

### Step 1 — Awareness
**"Where am I today?"**
The user sees goals, mood, and recent progress.

### Step 2 — Action
**"What should I do next?"**
Engage guides the user to focus on meaningful work and personal growth.

### Step 3 — Reflection
**"How do I feel about it?"**
The user records feelings or stress; the system learns and adjusts recommendations.

### Step 4 — Recognition
**"Am I seen?"**
Peers or managers recognize contributions instantly.

### Step 5 — Growth
**"What's next for me?"**
Engage provides direction for skill and career development.

This continuous cycle keeps engagement, happiness, and performance naturally aligned.
